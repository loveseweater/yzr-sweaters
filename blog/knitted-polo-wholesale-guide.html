Knitted Polo Shirts: A Wholesale Buyer's Guide

By YZR Sweaters · 2026-09

Views: 0

Knitted polos are one of the most reliable year-round categories in knitwear. They bridge smart and casual, work in spring collections as easily as in autumn layering, and suit men's, women's and unisex lines. If you buy knitwear wholesale, understanding what makes a good knit polo — and how to order one — will save you money and missed seasons.

Why knitted polos sell steadily

Unlike chunky winter sweaters, polos do not live in a single season. A mid-weight knit polo works as standalone wear in summer and as a layering piece in winter, which means steadier sell-through and less end-of-season discounting. For golf-adjacent, resort, smart-casual and workwear-adjacent labels, the polo is often the anchor item of the whole range.

Styles, collars and weights

  • Classic V-neck or crew with flat knit collar — the traditional golf-inspired look, usually mid-weight.
  • Half-zip or full-zip polo — a step up in perceived value, popular in menswear.
  • Fine-gauge female-fit polos — lighter yarns and slimmer silhouettes for womenswear lines.
  • Textured or structured knits — ottoman, pique-effect or rib structures that hold the collar and add visual interest.

Collar stability is the number-one quality marker in a knit polo. A collar that ripples after two washes signals loose linking or weak rib construction — check this on any sample before you commit.

Gauge and yarn choices

Most knitted polos sit between 12GG and 16GG. A 12GG polo in a cotton-acrylic blend feels substantial and holds shape; a 16GG fine-gauge version in viscose blend reads more dressed-up. Cotton and viscose blends dominate spring/summer programs, while wool blends move the same silhouette into autumn.

If you are unsure where to start, ask your knit polo manufacturer for two sample options in different gauges — the cost difference and hand feel will tell you quickly which fits your market.

Custom colours and private label

Polos are a colour-led category. Buyers typically order three to six colourways per style, so confirm that your supplier can dye to Pantone or match lab dips before you finalize the range. Private label details — woven main labels, printed care labels, hangtags and branded polybags — cost little but make the shirt unmistakably yours.

Our OEM/ODM service covers custom colours, private label trims and packaging on knit polo programs, with flexible MOQ so you can test colourways before scaling.

Planning your order

  • Confirm the silhouette on a fit sample, including collar and cuff recovery.
  • Approve lab dips for every colourway before bulk yarn is dyed.
  • Agree the size ratio — polos often sell in different ratios than sweaters.
  • Book production early for spring delivery; polo programs cluster in the same factory window as other fine-gauge knits.

Working with the right supplier

A knit polo lives or dies on collar quality, colour accuracy and finishing. Work with a supplier who sends pre-production photos, welcomes third-party inspection, and explains gauge and yarn trade-offs plainly.
