Why YZR

Built for brands that need a dependable knitwear partner

Established in Dongguan, the heart of China's garment and knitwear trade, YZR Sweaters connects reliable factory production with the sourcing needs of international buyers.

Who we are

YZR Sweaters is a knitwear manufacturer based in Dongguan, Guangdong, China. We design and produce custom knitwear — sweaters, cardigans, pullovers, knitted polos and knitted dresses — as OEM or ODM for fashion brands, wholesalers, importers and online retailers across Europe and North America.

Our team keeps things simple: clear communication, honest pricing, and garments that match the sample you approved. From the first inquiry to shipped cartons, you always know where your order stands.
