FAQ

Questions buyers ask us

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?
MOQ depends on the style and yarn. We keep it flexible and can often accommodate lower quantities for your first order or for sampling. Share your style and quantity to get a specific number.
Can you work from a design or reference photo?
Yes. We can develop samples from a sketch, a reference photo, a competitor sample, or a full tech pack. Our team will help translate your idea into production-ready specs.
Do you do private label and custom packaging?
Yes. We can add your brand labels, hang tags, and custom packaging (polybags, boxes, tissue) to fit your retail or e-commerce requirements.
What yarns and materials can you work with?
We work with a wide range including wool, cashmere blends, acrylic, cotton, viscose, and custom blends. We can advise on the best yarn for your target look, season and budget.
What are your lead times for sampling and bulk?
The sample and production time varies by style, quantity and season. We'll give you a clear schedule when we confirm the order so you can plan your launch.
How do I get a quote?
Send us your style, target quantity, yarn preference and any packaging requirements via the form below or by email. We'll reply with a quote and timeline.
Are you a factory or a trading company?
We are a Dongguan-based trading company that works with a network of certified knitwear factories. That means you get factory-direct production and pricing with added sourcing flexibility. Our partner factories hold BSCI, SEDEX and Oeko-Tex certification and welcome factory audits.
Can we visit or audit the factories?
Yes. Our production partners support factory audits (BSCI, SEDEX, Oeko-Tex) and welcome customer visits and third-party inspections, so you can verify our supply chain with confidence.
How quickly do you respond to inquiries?
We usually reply within one business day. For urgent inquiries, reach us on WhatsApp (+86 137 2449 4230) or email daniel@yzrsweaters.com.
