Knitwear buyers: how do you vet a new supplier before committing to a bulk order? 🧶

## ⑭ Quora（回答采购类问题）

**网址**：https://www.quora.com/
搜索这些问题回答（回答有帮助后再自然附上链接，别硬推）：
- "How do I find a good knitwear manufacturer?"
- "How to start a clothing brand?"
- "What is a reasonable MOQ for sweaters?"
- "How do I import sweaters from China?"

**回答模板**：
```
The most common mistake is choosing on price alone. What actually matters:

1. Confirm the supplier is real — business licence, factory walkthrough video, company records
2. Ask about certifications (BSCI, SEDEX, Oeko-Tex) and whether audits are welcome
3. Insist on a sample before bulk — judge hand feel, colour and fit in person
4. Agree the specs in writing: yarn, gauge, size chart, colour standard, packing
5. Confirm QC process and whether third-party inspection is allowed

I wrote a longer checklist here if useful: [link]
```

# 三条铁律（重复提醒）

1. ❌ **不要买外链**（淘宝/Fiverr 的"高质量外链"=站群，会被惩罚）
2. ❌ **不要群发论坛/评论区链接**
3. ✅ **要质量不要数量**：10 个真实相关外链 > 1000 个垃圾外链
