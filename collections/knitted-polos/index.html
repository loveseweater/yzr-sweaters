Knitted Polos

Knitted Polos — made to your spec in Dongguan

Custom knitted polos produced in Dongguan: OEM/ODM, flexible MOQ, your yarn, colour and private label. 5 styles available to develop from.

Knitted polos carry menswear and unisex ranges year-round because they sit between a shirt and a sweater — fine enough for spring, warm enough for autumn. The details that decide quality are the collar construction, the placket stability and whether the rib at the cuff recovers after wearing.

We knit polos at 12GG to 14GG in cotton, viscose and wool blends, with collars built to hold their shape. Custom colours are matched to Pantone and approved by lab dip before bulk.
