Cardigans & Knit Jackets

Cardigans & Knit Jackets — made to your spec in Dongguan

Custom cardigans & knit jackets produced in Dongguan: OEM/ODM, flexible MOQ, your yarn, colour and private label. 6 styles available to develop from.

Cardigans and knit jackets look simple on a hanger and are the most trim-dependent category we make. Buttons, zips, plackets and pocket placement all have to be specified, and every one of them is a separate line on the bill of materials — which is why cardigans are the category where "it looked cheaper than the sample" complaints usually start.

We specify closures, placket construction and rib trims line by line before bulk, and we approve them physically on a trim card. Bring us a button reference or a Pantone and we will source to it, or tell you honestly what the alternative costs.
