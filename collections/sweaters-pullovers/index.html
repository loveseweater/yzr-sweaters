Sweaters & Pullovers

Sweaters & Pullovers — made to your spec in Dongguan

Custom sweaters & pullovers produced in Dongguan: OEM/ODM, flexible MOQ, your yarn, colour and private label. 10 styles available to develop from.

Sweaters and pullovers are the backbone of most knitwear programmes, and they are also where specification sloppiness shows up first. A crew neck at 12GG in lambswool and the same shape at 7GG in an acrylic blend are two different products at two different prices — the silhouette looks similar in a photo and nothing else matches.

We knit both, and we will tell you which one suits your season and price point before you commit. Send a reference photo or tech pack, and we match yarn, gauge and fit in sampling until the sample is something you would put your own label in.
