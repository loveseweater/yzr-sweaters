Beanies & Hats

Beanies & Hats — made to your spec in Dongguan

Custom beanies & hats produced in Dongguan: OEM/ODM, flexible MOQ, your yarn, colour and private label. 5 styles available to develop from.

Beanies and knit hats are small, cheap to ship and the highest-margin accessory in most winter ranges. Because they are one size, they are also the lowest-risk product for a first order with a new supplier — a genuinely useful way to test quality and communication before placing a garment order.

We knit rib beanies, cuffed styles and pom hats in acrylic, wool and cotton, with custom Pantone colours and woven labels or patches applied to your spec.
