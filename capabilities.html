As a Dongguan knitwear manufacturer, we build long-term OEM/ODM relationships with fashion brands, wholesalers and online retailers. Here's what that means for your order.

01

Sample Development

We turn your reference, drawing or tech pack into a physical sample, refining gauge, fit and details until it's right.

  • In-house sampling
  • Yarn & gauge matching
  • Fit and sizing adjustment

02

OEM & ODM Production

Customize knitwear to your exact specs — yarn, color, label and packaging — with flexible order sizes.

  • Custom yarn & color
  • Private label & tags

03

Quality & Delivery

Consistent stitching, finishing and inspection so every carton meets your standard and your timeline.

  • Quality inspection
  • Reliable lead times
  • Export documentation

Our production process

A simple, transparent path from your first inquiry to a shipped order. You always know where things stand.

1

Share your spec

Send a style photo, tech pack, or a description with your target yarn, sizes and quantity.

2

Quote & sample

We confirm cost and timeline, then develop an approved sample for your review.

3

Bulk production

Once the sample is approved, we produce your order with in-process quality checks.

4

Ship & deliver

Final inspection, packing and export documentation — shipped to your port or doorstep.
