Why we show our own brand

A factory can claim anything about quality. Putting a brand we own and run on a marketplace where every review is public is a different kind of statement — it means our knitwear is judged by real customers, in the wash, not just by a buyer in a sample room. If you are weighing us as a manufacturing partner, the LINFAIR range is the most honest sample we can hand you.

The same lines behind LINFAIR make your OEM and private-label orders: Stoll and Shima Seiki flat knitting, in-house sampling, and QC drawn up around the return drivers that matter on marketplaces — pilling, shrinkage and sizing.
